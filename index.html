Who Should Check?

The strongest opportunities are typically established tipped businesses with meaningful payroll and card-tip volume.

Good candidates often include:

  • Restaurants, bars and hospitality businesses with tipped employees
  • Multiple locations or strong monthly sales volume
  • Employees whose tips are reported through payroll
  • Businesses that have not been consistently claiming the §45B credit

What we usually need to validate:

  • Forms 941 for the years being reviewed
  • Payroll reports showing hours and hourly wages
  • Tip detail by year
  • Confirmation whether the credit was previously claimed

Common Questions

My CPA already files our taxes. Why would I need BTA?

We specialize in the FICA Tip Credit calculation and work alongside your CPA. The goal is to identify an opportunity that may have been overlooked, not replace your tax professional.

Can prior years still qualify?

Potentially. Prior-year opportunities depend on the applicable filing deadlines and your tax situation, so timing matters.

Do you amend my income-tax return?

No. BTA prepares the FICA Tip Credit calculation package and Form 8846 support. Your CPA handles the appropriate income-tax filing or amendment.

Is every restaurant guaranteed a credit?

No. Eligibility and the amount depend on your payroll, tip reporting, wage information and prior filings.
