Who Should Request a Review?

Companies with recurring U.S. imports and meaningful duty payments are generally the most important businesses to evaluate.

Especially worth reviewing:

  • U.S. companies importing products or components
  • Businesses with regular ocean or air shipments
  • Companies sourcing heavily from countries affected by additional tariffs
  • Importers with substantial annual customs-duty expense
  • Businesses unsure whether prior duty payments were optimized or recoverable

Helpful information can include:

  • Importer of Record name
  • Customs entry information and entry summaries
  • HTS classifications
  • Countries of origin
  • Tariffs and duties paid
  • Customs broker or freight-forwarder records

Common Questions

Is every importer entitled to a tariff refund?

No. Eligibility depends on the specific imports, classifications, tariffs paid, entry history, deadlines and the legal mechanism available. We do not promise a refund before the records are reviewed.

Do I need to change my customs broker?

Not necessarily. The initial objective is to identify whether there is a potential recovery opportunity based on your company's import history.

What if I do not know how much duty we paid?

That is common. Start with the company and import information you know. Detailed records can be gathered if the initial review indicates a worthwhile opportunity.

Why should we review this now?

Customs matters can be deadline-sensitive. Waiting may affect which entries or recovery procedures remain available, so an early review can be important.
